# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive project.

Contributors must:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment;
- welcome people regardless of identity, background, experience, or location;
- give actionable feedback and assume good faith;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's confidential information;
- stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as harmful to the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, and retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security or private-contact mechanisms. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban
participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.
